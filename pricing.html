Common questions

Can I really enter all my information manually for free?

Yes. You can record essential household information, save your progress and download a usable binder. You won't need to pay to keep basic information organized.

Why pay for Family?

Family is intended for secure file uploads, advanced live sharing, convenient updates and household collaboration. During early access, currently implemented features remain free while we test the product.

Does Clarevo sell my email or assessment answers?

No. Clarevo may suggest optional educational resources or relevant services, such as estate-planning tools. We do not sell your email or assessment answers to partners. Paid referral relationships will be disclosed clearly.

Can I share my information with family?

You can download and share your binder yourself. Additional designated-contact sharing is available for testing during early access, with advanced live sharing planned for Family. Arrange access with trusted people in advance; online access cannot be guaranteed in every emergency.

What about sensitive information?

Don't enter passwords, SINs or full account numbers into free-text fields. Uploaded documents can contain sensitive information; we are continuing access-control and security testing before a wider launch. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

When will I be charged?

Not during early access. Checkout is disabled. We will publish renewal terms before enabling subscriptions; existing users won't be charged without choosing a paid plan.

Is Clarevo only for Canadians?

For now, Clarevo is designed for Canadian households. It helps organize information but does not provide legal, insurance or financial advice.
