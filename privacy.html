Privacy Policy

How Clarevo collects, uses, stores, and deletes information. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Who we are

Clarevo is an independently operated Canadian early-access service. We have not represented the service as incorporated. Our service helps Canadian households document their financial life and prepare their family for emergencies. You can reach us at hello@clarevo.ca.

What we collect and why

Clarevo processes information you choose to provide for assessment, planning, optional sharing, and document storage. Here's what that means in practice:

Information you provide directly

✓
Account information — your name and email address when you create an account.
✓
Assessment answers — your responses to the readiness questionnaire, used to calculate your score and identify gaps.
✓
Plan information — details you enter about your accounts, insurance, legal documents, and contacts. This is the core of your family plan.
✓
Uploaded documents — files you choose to upload such as your will or insurance policy. Files are stored in a private storage bucket. Uploaded files can include sensitive personal information; avoid uploading documents you do not need for your emergency plan.

💡 Keep sensitive credentials out: Clarevo is an emergency binder, not a password manager. Do not enter passwords, social insurance numbers or full financial credentials into free-text fields. Documents you choose to upload may themselves contain sensitive information.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure database platform that uses industry-standard encryption. Our configured Supabase project is hosted in the United States. Data processed by our infrastructure and service providers may therefore be accessed or stored outside Canada, subject to applicable laws.

We use authenticated access controls and database row-level security policies to restrict access to saved plans and documents. Authorized service administrators may access data when required to operate, secure, support or investigate incidents involving Clarevo. No technical safeguard can guarantee absolute security.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal information. We do not sell assessment answers or contact details to referral partners. Service providers process limited data to operate Clarevo, including analytics where enabled.

We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

Limited sharing

1
People you authorize — if you share your plan with a spouse or executor, they can access only the sections you've enabled.
2
Service providers — Supabase handles authentication, database and file storage. PostHog provides website and product analytics, such as page views and assessment engagement. These providers process relevant information to operate and improve Clarevo.
3
Legal requirements — if required by Canadian law or a valid court order.

Partner links and referrals

Clarevo may include links to third-party services such as Willful and Wealthsimple. These links may include tracking parameters that help us measure how many users click through, so we can demonstrate the value we provide to partners. We do not automatically provide your assessment answers or email address to referral partners. Clicking a partner link may disclose your IP address, browser information and referral parameters to that website, which operates under its own privacy policy. Any paid referral relationship will be disclosed.

Cookies

Clarevo uses authentication-related browser storage to keep you signed in and PostHog to understand website and product usage. PostHog may use cookies or other browser storage depending on its configuration. We do not currently use advertising cookies to serve personalized ads. You can use browser privacy settings to restrict cookies. We are reviewing our analytics configuration and applicable consent requirements.

Your rights under PIPEDA

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law. Signed-in users can initiate account deletion in Dashboard → Account Settings. You can also contact hello@clarevo.ca for assistance. We aim to respond to privacy inquiries promptly; response periods and exceptions depend on applicable law.

Privacy rights differ by jurisdiction, including additional rights under Quebec privacy law. Contact us if you have a request relating to access, portability, correction or withdrawal of consent.

Data retention

We retain saved plan information and uploads while your account is active, unless you remove them earlier. The self-service account deletion workflow attempts to remove your uploaded files, saved records, sharing permissions and authentication account, and reports failure rather than claiming deletion succeeded. Information may persist temporarily in backups, security logs or service-provider retention systems, subject to their retention schedules and legal requirements. We cannot guarantee removal of copies you previously downloaded or shared. Aggregated, de-identified analytics may be retained.

Security

We use HTTPS, encryption at rest supported by our hosting provider, authentication and access policies. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; Clarevo does not store users' plaintext passwords. Our systems remain under security review and may contain vulnerabilities.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe there has been a security incident affecting your data, contact us immediately at hello@clarevo.ca.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we'll notify you by email or with a notice in the app. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email us at hello@clarevo.ca. We're a small team and we'll respond personally.

⚠️ Note: This privacy policy has been prepared in good faith for a Canadian audience. It is not legal advice. This policy should be reviewed by a qualified Canadian privacy lawyer before a broader public launch, including obligations under PIPEDA and applicable provincial laws.
